## 1. What this Policy covers

This Policy explains how Ezer, operated by **EzerLab LLC**, uses cookies, local storage, session identifiers, and similar technologies. See the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/) for broader personal-information practices.

## 2. Necessary and functional technologies

The following support features a user requests and generally do not require optional analytics consent:

- **Sign-in and authentication** to maintain a session, verify identity, and protect accounts;
- **security and quotas** to prevent misuse, enforce access limits, and maintain the Service;
- **theme and language** to remember display preferences;
- **cookie choice** to remember whether you accepted optional analytics; and
- **referral source** to remember a code that arrived with an intentional visit, where applicable.

Blocking these technologies can prevent sign-in, preferences, or product features from working correctly.

## 3. Optional analytics

We actively load the following non-essential tools only after you select "Accept analytics":

- **Google Analytics** for aggregate visits, pages, devices, and performance;
- **Microsoft Clarity** to understand interface interaction and errors; and
- **Baidu Analytics** for traffic trends and site performance.

These providers may receive technical data such as IP address, device, browser, page URL, events, and approximate location, and process it under their own policies.

## 4. Hosting and security logs

Hosting and content-delivery providers such as Vercel and Cloudflare may process required technical logs for delivery, security, anti-fraud, troubleshooting, and aggregate performance. The optional analytics control does not fully govern these necessary provider activities.

## 5. Your choices

On the first visit, you may:

- select "Accept analytics" to allow optional analytics;
- select "Reject optional analytics" to keep only necessary and functional technology; or
- reopen the panel at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Withdrawing consent prevents our future active loading of optional analytics, but may not automatically delete data a third party previously set. You can also remove cookies and site data through browser settings.

## 6. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to inspect, block, or delete cookies and local storage. Blocking all site storage may sign you out, reset theme preferences, and affect functionality.

There is no uniform industry standard for "Do Not Track" signals. We will continue to evaluate applicable legal and technical requirements.

## 7. Duration

Storage duration depends on purpose, provider configuration, and browser settings. We seek to limit duration to what is needed for functionality, performance analysis, and legal obligations.

## 8. Changes and contact

We will update this Policy and date if tools or purposes materially change. Questions may be sent to [contact@ezer.cc](mailto:contact@ezer.cc).
