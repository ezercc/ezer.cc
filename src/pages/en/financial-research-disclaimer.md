## 1. General information

Ezer, operated by **EzerLab LLC**, is software for organizing public company disclosures, structuring financial data, and supporting financial-statement research. Website content, assessments, risk markers, charts, reports, and model outputs are for general information, education, and research.

## 2. No investment advice

EzerLab is not an investment adviser or broker-dealer. Nothing in the Service is:

- a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or trade a security or other asset;
- personalized portfolio advice, a price target, a return promise, or a suitability determination;
- a promise about future performance, market direction, or investment outcome; or
- an offering, underwriting, trade-execution, or customer-asset custody service.

Interface labels such as "assessment," "risk," "trusted," or "suspect" describe research process or data quality and are not securities ratings.

## 3. No statutory audit or professional opinion

"Review," "adversarial verification," and historical references to "audit-style" describe an internal software research method. They do not mean a CPA audit, statutory audit, assurance engagement, audit opinion, or certification of financial statements.

EzerLab does not provide legal, tax, accounting, or other licensed professional advice.

## 4. Data and model limitations

The Service may use company disclosures, regulatory filings, third-party data, automated parsing, rules, and models. Public or authoritative sources may still involve:

- reporting delays, corrections, or differences in definitions;
- table-parsing, unit, currency, mapping, or translation errors;
- source coverage or licensing limits;
- model omissions, faulty reasoning, stale information, or uncertainty; and
- expired links or changing third-party content.

Source links and evidence cues support review but do not guarantee complete, accurate, or current outputs.

## 5. Independent verification

Before acting on an output, you should:

- read the company's original regulatory filings and notes;
- verify the period, units, currency, accounting standard, and corrections;
- consider your own purpose, risk tolerance, and legal duties; and
- consult a qualified independent professional when appropriate.

## 6. Historical and forward-looking information

Past performance does not indicate future results. Any inference, scenario, or trend description is uncertain and should not be treated as an earnings forecast or return guarantee.

## 7. Independence and third parties

Unless expressly disclosed, EzerLab does not represent an analyzed company, exchange, regulator, data terminal, or model provider. Third-party names and marks belong to their owners.
