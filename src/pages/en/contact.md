## Operator

**EzerLab LLC** is a Wyoming limited liability company that operates Ezer financial-statement research software and ezer.cc.

Any registered-agent or legal-mail address is used for company registration and formal mail and is not represented as the location of day-to-day operations. Website support is handled primarily through the email below.

## Customer support email

**[joe@ezerlab.org](mailto:joe@ezerlab.org)**

Contact us about:

- account access, sign-in, or quota;
- product features, sources, or report feedback;
- Premium early access;
- billing, refunds, cancellation, or digital delivery;
- privacy requests, cookie choices, or data security;
- website ownership, business inquiries, or legal notices.

## Helpful information

To help us respond, include:

- the account email;
- the complete URL of the affected page;
- company name, code, and report period for research issues;
- order or payment identifier for billing issues; and
- a description and necessary screenshot.

Do not send a full card number, bank password, account password, one-time code, passport, or identity document by ordinary email unless we specifically request it through a secure channel and explain why.

## Response time

We normally respond within **2 business days**. We prioritize security, unauthorized-payment, and non-delivery reports. Timing may vary with holidays, complexity, and third-party investigations.

## Languages

Support is available in English and Chinese. Write in whichever language is more convenient.

## Company and product boundaries

EzerLab provides software and general financial-statement research information. It does not provide personalized investment advice, securities brokerage, trade execution, custody of customer funds, lending, money transmission, or statutory audit services.
